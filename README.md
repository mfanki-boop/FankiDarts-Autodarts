# 🎯 FankiDarts for Autodarts

**Make Autodarts your game!**

FankiDarts is a feature-rich Tampermonkey userscript that enhances [Autodarts](https://play.autodarts.com) with additional match features, UI customization, camera effects, statistics, social tools and external device integrations.

## ✨ Features

- **Match UI Customization** – Adjust layouts, scaling, backgrounds and visual effects.
- **Checkout Zoom & Highlight** – Camera zoom on successful checkouts and visual checkout guidance.
- **Player Cards** – Customized player card visuals.
- **Friends & Boo Boys** – Highlight and tag other players.
- **Bot Boards, Bot Darts & Bot Attitude** – Customize bot matches and reactions.
- **Voice Control** – Control supported Autodarts actions with voice commands.
- **Chat Tools** – Automatic messages, custom buttons and chat filtering.
- **Leg History & Tournaments** – Additional information directly on the Autodarts home screen.
- **PDC Live Information** – Follow professional darts events.
- **AWTRIX3 Integration** – Display scores and animated GIFs on compatible 32×8 LED displays.
- **GIF Gallery & Dart Puppets** – Fun visual extras.
- **Notifications** – Optional smartphone push notifications via ntfy.

Most features can be enabled or disabled individually in the FankiDarts settings. A detailed manual is included in the script.

## 🚀 Installation

1. Install [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) in your browser.
2. Open the [FankiDarts page on Greasy Fork](https://greasyfork.org/de/scripts/573373-fankidarts-for-autodarts-v2).
3. Click **Install this script** and confirm the installation.
4. Open [Autodarts](https://play.autodarts.com) and configure FankiDarts.

**Updates are distributed through Greasy Fork and Tampermonkey.**

## 💻 Compatibility

FankiDarts is primarily developed for X01 matches on PC and Mac, optimized for 1920×1080 displays. Chrome is recommended. Mobile devices are not specifically supported.
